Panera Bread sandwiches and paninis

The sandwich board is the busiest part of the Panera Bread menu. Hot, toasted sandwiches like the Toasted Frontega Chicken (about $11.79), Chipotle Chicken Avo Melt ($11.99), Spicy Fiesta Chicken ($11.99) and Toasted Italiano ($12.99) are pressed on Panera's focaccia or ciabatta. Cold classics such as the Bacon Turkey Bravo ($11.99), Turkey & Cheddar ($10.29) and Tomato Basil BLT ($9.69) are built on the house breads. The lowest-priced sandwiches are the Mediterranean Veggie and Grilled Cheese at about $7.99.

Panera Bread soups, bread bowls and mac

Every soup on the Panera Bread menu costs about $8.49. That includes Broccoli Cheddar, Creamy Tomato, Chicken Noodle, French Onion, Chicken & Wild Rice, Baked Potato, Fireside Chili and the seasonal Autumn Squash. A bread bowl version is about $9.08, and you can even buy empty sourdough bread bowls from the bakery for about $2.89 to fill at home. Mac & Cheese is about $10.59, or $11.79 with bacon.

Panera Bread salads and grain bowls

Salads run from about $8.99 for a plain Caesar or Greek to $13.59 for the Green Goddess Chicken Cobb. Most chicken salads, including the Fuji Apple, Asian Sesame and Greek with Chicken, are about $11.99. The Greens with Grains bowls cost about $9.99 without chicken or $12.99 with it.

Panera Bread breakfast menu

In the morning, the Panera Bread menu switches to bagel stacks ($6.99), ciabatta and croissant egg sandwiches ($6.89), soufflés ($6.99), avocado toast ($3.89), steel cut oatmeal ($6.19) and yogurt parfaits ($5.89). Breakfast times and the full morning line-up are covered on the Panera Bread Breakfast Menu page.

Panera Bread Value Duets

Value Duets are the best deal on the Panera Bread menu. You get a smaller sandwich or salad plus a soup for about $7.99, which is less than most full sandwiches on their own. Pairings include Grilled Cheese, Smokehouse BBQ Chicken, Mediterranean Veggie or Tuna Salad with Creamy Tomato Soup, and a Caesar Salad with Chicken Noodle Soup. The meal finder above can show Value Duet options only.

Panera Bread drinks

Fountain drinks, iced tea and Agave Lemonade are about $3.39. Flavoured lemonades are about $4.29, hot coffee about $2.99, lattes $4.59–$5.19 and smoothies about $6.49. Regular drinkers can join the Unlimited Sip Club for about $14.99 a month or $99 a year. The habit calculator above shows what that saves on your own order.

How to order from the Panera Bread menu

  • In the app or online for pickup or delivery. Pickup avoids delivery-app markups.
  • Rapid Pick-Up lets you order ahead and grab your food from a shelf in the cafe.
  • MyPanera is the free rewards program. Members get offers and a birthday treat.
  • Kiosks and the counter in the cafe, where you can customise breads, sauces and toppings.

Panera Bread menu for every time of day

Morning: bagel stacks, egg sandwiches, soufflés, oatmeal and a coffee. Lunch: a sandwich or salad, or a Value Duet if you want soup too. Afternoon snack: a cookie, a pastry or a bagel with cream cheese. Dinner: a bread bowl, Mac & Cheese or a Family Feast to share. Because so much of the Panera Bread menu is soup, bread and salad, it is one of the easier fast-casual menus for mixing light and hearty choices in the same order.

Lighter picks on the Panera Bread menu

If you want something lighter, look at the Mediterranean Veggie sandwich, the Greek or Caesar salads, the Greens with Grains bowls, the Garden Avo & Egg White Sandwich, steel cut oatmeal, the fruit cup or an apple as your side. Panera lists full nutrition for every item in its app and in the cafe, so you can check the details before you order.

Seasonal items

The Panera Bread menu changes a little through the year. Seasonal soups such as Autumn Squash return in the cooler months, and new sandwiches, salads and drinks are added from time to time. The core favourites, like Broccoli Cheddar, the Bacon Turkey Bravo and the bagel line-up, stay all year. The date at the top of this page shows when the prices were last checked.

Why Panera Bread prices differ

Panera bakery-cafes are run by the company and by franchise partners, and prices follow local costs. Airport, city-centre and delivery-app prices are usually the highest. The prices on this page are typical U.S. prices, last checked in November 2026.

Panera Bread menu FAQ

Straight answers about Panera Bread prices and the 2026 menu.

What is on the Panera Bread menu?

The Panera Bread menu includes breakfast bagel stacks, egg sandwiches and soufflés, hot and cold sandwiches, soups and bread bowls, Mac & Cheese, salads and grain bowls, Value Duets, bagels, cookies, pastries and whole breads, a kids menu, catering and drinks.

How much does a meal at Panera Bread cost?

A sandwich with a drink usually costs about $10 to $20 before tax, and around $15 for a typical order like a Bacon Turkey Bravo with Agave Lemonade. The cheapest full lunch is a Value Duet (sandwich or salad plus soup) for about $7.99.

What is the cheapest thing on the Panera Bread menu?

The cheapest items are a French Baguette side for about $0.99, chips or an apple for about $1.39, and bagels for about $1.99. The cheapest meal is a Value Duet at about $7.69 to $7.99.

How much is soup at Panera Bread?

Panera soups cost about $8.49, or about $9.08 in a sourdough bread bowl. Quarts for groups are about $21.99.

Does Panera Bread sell whole loaves of bread?

Yes. Whole breads include the French Baguette (about $3.99), Country Rustic Sourdough ($8.79), Tomato Basil ($8.19) and Classic White Miche ($10.39).

Does Panera Bread do catering?

Yes. Group options include soups by the quart (about $21.99), a Coffee Box ($21.99), half gallons of lemonade ($7.29–$9.49), Family Feasts from about $36 and a Baked Goods Feast ($16.99).

How can I save money at Panera Bread?

Choose a Value Duet, buy bagels on Tuesday, join the Unlimited Sip Club if you buy drinks often, sign up for MyPanera rewards and order ahead for pickup instead of using delivery apps.
